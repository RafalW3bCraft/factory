Pocketful Factory is a four-seat autonomous software factory for the pocketful challenge.
The real goal is to preserve the correctness of a wallet and peer-to-peer payments system while preventing bad funds movement and verifying all changes independently.
The required BAND Desktop room record must be captured separately in the actual room. This script marks the slot explicitly because it is a required human action and cannot be simulated.
The factory uses four seats: Foreman, Smith, Inspector, and Stresser. Each seat is governed by generic mandates, and the mandate lint check confirms the work remains track-agnostic.
The shipped work is Stage 1. It constructs cleanly in Docker, runs in an isolated container with restricted network access, and returns a valid health response from the service.
The HTTP checks include /health and the reset behavior. A valid fixture returns 204, while an empty-body request returns 400, showing the service rejects malformed input.
The defect story is honest: Stresser found a concern about empty-body handling, Smith fixed it and also found a bonus cold-start issue, and Inspector accepted the deltaed revision after verification.
The room log also contains four human messages, three OpenCode timeouts, and roughly a 1h55m stall, which must be disclosed to the judges as part of the autonomy accounting.
The final submission cannot claim a complete room-recording pass until the human records the actual BAND desktop room. The repo remains honest: Stage 2 remains draft-only, stages 3 and 4 are not reached, and the final form and spend figure are still pending human action.
