Pocketful Factory is a four-seat software factory built for a wallet and peer-to-peer payments challenge.

For payments, correctness means more than a successful response. Balances must conserve funds, and malformed requests must fail safely.

Foreman coordinates, Smith implements, Inspector independently verifies, and Stresser probes resilience. Defects return to Smith for a verified follow-up.

This real Band room excerpt shows the review request, Inspector's acceptance, then a hardening order to Stresser. Workstation paths are masked.

After that acceptance, Stresser flagged empty-body handling. Smith fixed it and independently found a cold-start index defect; Inspector accepted the delta.

Stage One passed 147 of 147 official checks. An independent review repeated the pass; separate private smoke suites passed 145 of 145.

The run needed four human messages, included three OpenCode timeouts, and stalled for about one hour and fifty-five minutes.

Stage One shipped. Stage Two was not committed to main; Stages Three and Four were not reached. This is the verified boundary of the result.

Pocketful Factory shows how handoffs, adversarial tests, and independent review can catch defects, while its record stays honest about human input.
